**Related:** [Legal Center](/legal) · [Terms](/terms) · [Privacy](/privacy)

  
## Do not use CuePoint Planning to:

  
- Commit fraud, money laundering, or other illegal activity
- Harass, abuse, threaten, or defame anyone
- Upload malware or attempt to disrupt the service
- Probe, scan, or attack our systems or other accounts
- Scrape or bulk-extract data except through features we give you for your own account
- Impersonate a person or misrepresent your affiliation
- Resell, sublicense, or white-label the service without written permission
- Bypass subscription, trial, or security limits
- Store or transmit content you lack rights to process (including client data without a lawful basis)
- Use AI features to generate unlawful, infringing, or deceptive client materials without human review
- Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages with our email or automation tools
