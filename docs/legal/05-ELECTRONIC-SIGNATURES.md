# Electronic Signatures Notice

*Last updated: August 13, 2026 · ESIGN / UETA*

  

  
> CuePoint Planning’s client portal lets DJs and their event clients sign contracts electronically. This notice explains what that means.

  
**Note:** CuePoint Planning provides software only. We are not a law firm and are not a party to contracts between DJs and their clients. Have counsel review your templates for your state and event type.

  
**Related:** [Legal Center](/legal) · [Terms](/terms) · [Privacy](/privacy)

  
## 1. Consent to do business electronically

  
By completing the portal signing flow, you agree to conduct that transaction electronically and that your electronic signature — including a typed name plus click-to-sign or similar affirmation — may sign the displayed agreement.

  
## 2. Legal effect

  
Under the U.S. Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN) and applicable state Uniform Electronic Transactions Acts (UETA), electronic signatures and records can have the same legal effect as ink-on-paper signatures when the parties consent and other legal requirements are met.

  
## 3. What CuePoint may record

  
When a contract is signed in the portal, we may store the signer’s name, signature affirmation, timestamp, and available technical metadata (such as IP address or user agent) with the contract record on the DJ’s account.

  
## 4. Copies & withdrawal

  
Ask the DJ who sent the contract for a copy, or export/print from the portal when available. If you do not want to sign electronically, do not complete the signing flow — contact the other party to use another method.

  
## 5. DJ responsibilities

  
- Ensure contract content is accurate and lawful
- Obtain any additional consents your jurisdiction requires
- Do not use portal e-sign for documents that must be notarized or that law requires on paper
