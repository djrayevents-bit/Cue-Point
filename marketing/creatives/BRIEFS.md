# Creative briefs — SIGNAL × CuePoint

Use these specs when making graphics/Reels. Keep brand cohesive.

## Brand tokens (from product)

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Accent | `#6C4DF6` / pink `#E91E8C` / purple `#7B2FBE` / cyan `#4DD9D9` |
| Ink | `#16161A` / charcoal `#2D2D3A` |
| Tagline | The business behind the music. |
| Wordmark | CuePoint Planning |

**Visual rules:** Big type. One idea. High contrast. No cluttered dashboards as the hero — show one UI moment or bold typography. Avoid generic stock "DJ with headphones smiling at laptop."

---

## Recurring templates

### 1) Countdown card (use Jul 22–31 PM)
- Full-bleed dark charcoal
- Giant number (10…1) in white
- Subline: `AUGUST 1` in accent gradient
- Footer: CuePoint wordmark + "Join the list"
- Size: 1080×1350 (IG) + 1080×1920 (Stories/TikTok)

### 2) Founder pricing lock
- Stamp: `FOUNDER PRICING · FIRST 25`
- Big `$20/mo` → struck or arrow to `$50/mo`
- CTA bar at bottom
- Size: 1080×1080 and 1080×1350

### 3) Pain punch (AM Reels cover)
- One sentence hook, max 8 words on screen
- Hard cut within 1.5s
- End card always: CuePoint + date/CTA
- Length: 7–15 seconds

### 4) Feature carousel
- Slide 1 = hook only (no logo dump)
- Middle = one feature per slide
- Last = CTA + URL
- 1080×1350

### 5) Launch sting (Aug 1 AM)
- 0–2s: black + waveform
- 2–5s: product flashes (events, portal, contract signed)
- 5–8s: **WE'RE LIVE**
- 8–12s: Start free trial + URL
