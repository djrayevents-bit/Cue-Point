**Codename:** SIGNAL

**Mission:** Steal attention. Convert DJs. Fill the Founder 25 before August 15.  
**Push starts:** August 1, 2026  
**Cadence:** 3 posts/day from Aug 1 through launch, then hold the line.  
**Primary CTA (pre-launch):** Join the launch list → cuepointplanning.com  
**Primary CTA (Aug 15+):** Start free trial → cuepointplanning.com/app#signup  
**Offer:** First 25 DJs lock $20/mo forever (goes to $50/mo after). 30-day free trial.

---

## Who we are talking to

**Primary:** Working DJs (weddings, corporate, school, private) who book gigs but run the business on Notes app, texts, Google Sheets, and hope.

**Secondary:** Multi-DJ shops / small entertainment companies drowning in logistics.

**Mindset:** Hustle-proud, allergic to corporate SaaS speak, hate wasted admin time, want to look pro in front of clients.

---

## Voice: intense, not loud

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short punches. One idea per post. | Soft "we're excited to announce" |
| Call out the pain by name (spreadsheets, lost leads, unsigned contracts) | Feature dump without a wound |
| Speak like a DJ who built the tool | Speak like a startup pitch deck |
| Scarcity that's real (Founder 25, Aug 15) | Fake countdown clocks every day |
| Direct CTA every PM post | Vague "link in bio" with no reason |
| Specifics: events, CRM, contracts, music, portal, invoices, CUE | Vague "all-in-one solution" |

**Tone words:** sharp, confident, street-smart, urgent, pro.  
**Forbidden:** revolutionary, disrupt, synergy, game-changer, excited to share, community (overused).

## Daily rhythm (3 slots)

| Slot | Time (local) | Job | Format bias |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AM** | 8:00 | Interrupt the scroll. Pattern break. | Reel / TikTok / single image |
| **MID** | 12:30 | Prove the wound or the win. | Carousel / story thread |
| **PM** | 18:00 | Convert. Hard CTA. | Static + caption, Stories sticker |

Every day ends with a signup ask. No "engagement bait only" days.

## Content pillars (rotate)

1. **Pain** — Chaos of running a DJ business without a system  
2. **Proof** — Feature moments that feel like relief  
3. **Status** — Looking pro to clients (portal, contracts, brand)  
4. **Money** — Invoices, profit, Founder pricing scarcity  
5. **Countdown** — August 15 launch pressure  
6. **Identity** — "Built by DJs, for DJs"

---

## Conversion rules

1. AM can tease. MID can educate. **PM must ask.**  
2. Every CTA names the next action: *join the list* or *start free trial*.  
3. Founder pricing mentioned at least 1×/day from Jul 27 onward.  
4. Never post without a clear intent tag in the calendar (ATTENTION / DESIRE / URGENCY / CONVERT).  
5. Hashtags: max 5–8, niche-first (`#WeddingDJ` `#DJBusiness` `#EventDJ`), not spam walls.
