Privacy notice

Last updated September 9, 2026

CuePoint is a private system for running a DJ / events business. It is not a commercial multi-user product. This notice covers data the operator stores to deliver that business.

What is stored

  • Operator account (email or phone used to sign in)
  • Client and lead contacts, events, contracts, invoices, music plans, and related files the operator enters
  • Information clients submit through portal, booking, questionnaire, or meeting links the operator shares

Processors

Infrastructure may include Supabase (database/auth), Vercel (hosting), Resend (transactional email), Anthropic (AI assistant used by the operator), Spotify (track search), and Google Calendar (meetings, if connected). Card payments for CuePoint itself are not collected. Client invoice payment details are not stored as full card numbers.

Sharing

Client data is not sold. It is used to plan and deliver events. Portal and booking links are secret URLs; anyone with a valid link can view that event’s client-facing information.

Contact / deletion

Email ivstudiogroup@gmail.com to ask about access or deletion of your client information.
