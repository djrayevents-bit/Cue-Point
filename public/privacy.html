1. Who We Are

CuePoint Planning (“CuePoint,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by CuePoint Planning LLC, a Rhode Island limited liability company. The platform is available at cuepointplanning.com.

Privacy and data requests: support@cuepointplanning.com

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account & authentication: name, email address, and/or phone number; one-time passcodes (OTP) sent by email or SMS (we do not use account passwords)
  • Billing email: if you sign up with SMS first, a billing email may be required for Stripe checkout
  • DJ profile: business name, DJ name, phone, address, website, branding (logo, colors)
  • Business data you enter: events, leads, clients, venues, contracts, invoices, packages, timelines, questionnaires, music plans, gear/wardrobe notes, and similar CRM content
  • Financial records you track: invoice amounts, deposits, payment methods you log (Venmo, Zelle, etc.). We do not store full payment card numbers — Stripe processes cards
  • Communications: support requests, feedback, and feature interest notifications

Information collected automatically

  • Browser, device, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited, features used, session timing
  • Error and diagnostic logs needed to keep the service reliable

Information from service providers

  • Stripe: subscription status, customer IDs, and billing events (card data stays with Stripe)
  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting
  • Resend: delivery of transactional email
  • Spotify: music search results when you use in-app search (we do not store your Spotify login)
  • Anthropic: processes prompts you submit to CUE / AI features
  • Google (optional): Calendar / Meet connection if you enable scheduling integrations
  • Vercel / hosting CDN: request logs for delivery and security

See our Subprocessors list for a current vendor overview.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve CuePoint Planning
  • Authenticate you via email or SMS one-time codes
  • Process subscriptions and manage billing via Stripe
  • Send transactional messages (OTP codes, billing receipts, welcome/support email)
  • Power client portal features you enable for your event clients
  • Run AI-assisted features when you choose to use them
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your CRM or client data for third-party advertising.

4. Client Portal Data

When you share a portal link, your clients may submit music requests, questionnaire answers, signatures, and related event details. That information is stored in connection with your account so you can run the event.

You are responsible for telling your clients why you are collecting their information and for having any consent required by law. CuePoint processes portal submissions only to provide the features you enable. See also our Electronic Signatures notice.

5. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

If a privacy law requires a “legal basis,” we typically rely on: (a) performing our contract with you; (b) our legitimate interests in operating a secure SaaS product; (c) your consent (for example, SMS OTP); and (d) legal obligations (tax and accounting records).

6. Data Storage and Security

Primary application data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL on cloud infrastructure). We use HTTPS in transit, access controls, expiring auth tokens, and database security controls including row-level policies where configured.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and business data: retained while your account is active
  • After cancellation or deletion request: typically retained up to 30 days, then deleted or anonymized, unless we must keep records longer
  • Billing and tax records: may be retained up to 7 years as required
  • Security logs: retained for a limited period for abuse prevention

8. Sharing Your Information

We share data only with:

  • Service providers / subprocessors listed in Subprocessors
  • Legal requirements when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business transfers (merger or acquisition), with notice where required

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies and browser storage (including localStorage) for authentication and app performance. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

10. SMS and Phone Numbers

If you choose SMS authentication, we process your phone number to send one-time sign-in codes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent details and opt-out instructions are in our SMS / OTP Terms.

11. AI Features

When you use CUE or other AI tools, your prompts and relevant context may be sent to our AI provider (currently Anthropic) to generate a response. Do not submit secrets you are not comfortable processing through that provider. Review AI output before sending it to clients. See the AI section of our Terms.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to legal retention needs
  • Export data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Appeal or escalate a privacy request where local law provides that right

Email support@cuepointplanning.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

California / similar US state laws: We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly defined. You may still exercise access, deletion, and correction rights by contacting us.

13. Children’s Privacy

CuePoint Planning is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us to delete any account created by a minor.

14. International Users

We are based in the United States. If you access CuePoint from another country, your information may be processed in the U.S. and other locations where our providers operate.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above. For material changes, we will provide email or in-app notice when practical. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
