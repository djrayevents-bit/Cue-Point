1. Who We Are

CuePoint Planning (“CuePoint,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by CuePoint Planning LLC. The service is available at cuepointplanning.com.

2. Who This Policy Covers

  • DJ / business users who create CuePoint accounts
  • Visitors to our marketing site
  • Event clients who use a DJ’s client portal link (music requests, questionnaires, e-sign, and similar)

If you are an event client, the DJ who invited you is typically the business collecting your information for their event. CuePoint processes that data to provide the portal features the DJ enables.

3. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account & authentication: name, email, and/or mobile number; one-time passcodes (OTP) by email or SMS. CuePoint uses passwordless login — we do not store an account password.
  • Billing email: required for Stripe checkout (including when you first authenticate by SMS)
  • DJ profile: business name, DJ name, phone, address, website, branding assets
  • Business content you enter: events, leads, clients, venues, contracts, invoices, packages, timelines, questionnaires, music plans, gear notes, and related CRM data
  • Payment tracking you log: amounts, dates, and methods such as Venmo or Zelle. Full payment card numbers for CuePoint subscriptions are handled by Stripe — we do not store them.
  • Support & feedback: messages you send us

Information collected automatically

  • Browser, device, and OS
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages and features used; session timing
  • Diagnostic and error logs needed for reliability and security

Information from service providers

  • Stripe — subscription status, customer IDs, billing events
  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Spotify — music search results when you use in-app search
  • Anthropic — prompts you send to CUE / AI features
  • Google (optional) — Calendar / Meet data if you connect scheduling
  • Hosting / CDN (Vercel) — request logs for delivery and abuse prevention

Current vendor overview: Subprocessors.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve CuePoint Planning
  • Authenticate you with email or SMS one-time codes
  • Process subscriptions and manage billing through Stripe
  • Send transactional messages (OTP codes, receipts, product/account email)
  • Power client portal features your DJ account enables
  • Run AI-assisted features when you choose to use them
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your CRM or client data for third-party advertising.

5. Client Portal Data

Portal submissions (music requests, questionnaire answers, signatures, and related details) are stored so the inviting DJ can run the event. DJs are responsible for telling their clients why information is collected and for any consents required by law. See also our Electronic Signatures Notice.

6. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Where a privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our contract with you; legitimate interests in running a secure SaaS product; consent (for example, SMS OTP); and legal obligations such as tax recordkeeping.

7. Data Storage and Security

Application data is primarily stored with Supabase (cloud PostgreSQL). We use HTTPS in transit, access controls, expiring authentication tokens, and database security controls including row-level policies where configured.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

8. Retention

  • Active accounts: retained while the account is open
  • After cancellation or deletion request: typically deleted or anonymized within about 30 days, unless law requires longer retention
  • Billing / tax records: may be kept up to 7 years
  • Security logs: kept for a limited period for abuse prevention

9. Sharing

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers / subprocessors listed in Subprocessors
  • Legal requirements when required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business transfers (merger or acquisition), with notice where required

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies and browser storage (including localStorage) for authentication and app performance. Details: Cookie Policy.

11. SMS and Phone Numbers

If you choose SMS authentication, we process your phone number to send one-time sign-in codes. Message and data rates may apply. Consent and opt-out (STOP / HELP): SMS / OTP Terms.

12. AI Features

When you use CUE or other AI tools, your prompts and related context may be sent to our AI provider (currently Anthropic) to generate a response. Review all AI output before sending it to clients. Do not submit secrets or data you are not authorized to process. See the AI section of our Terms.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete data, subject to legal retention needs
  • Export data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Email support@cuepointplanning.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We aim to respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity.

U.S. state privacy laws (including California): We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly defined. You may still exercise access, deletion, and correction rights by contacting us.

14. Children

CuePoint Planning is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us to remove any account created by a minor.

15. International Users

We are based in the United States. If you use CuePoint from another country, your information may be processed in the U.S. and other locations where our providers operate.

16. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy and will revise the “Last updated” date above. For material changes, we will provide email or in-app notice when practical. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
