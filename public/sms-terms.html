SMS / OTP Terms

Last updated: August 13, 2026

These terms cover text messages used to sign in to CuePoint Planning. They supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
Legal Center Privacy Terms

1. Program

CuePoint Planning account verification — transactional SMS containing one-time passcodes (OTP) so you can create an account or sign in without a password. These are not marketing messages.

2. Consent

By providing your mobile number and requesting an SMS code, you consent to receive automated text messages from CuePoint Planning and our authentication providers for verification and account security. Consent is not a condition of buying any other product, but SMS login will not work without a reachable number.

3. Frequency

Message frequency varies. You typically receive a message when you request a signup or sign-in code, and occasionally for related security notices.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. CuePoint does not charge a separate SMS fee.

5. Opt out & help

  • Reply STOP to cancel SMS from that sender
  • Reply HELP for help
  • Or email support@cuepointplanning.com and switch to email OTP

After opting out, SMS login may stop until you re-consent by requesting a new code.

6. Carriers

Delivery is not guaranteed on every carrier or in every region. We are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages caused by carriers or your device.

7. Privacy

We use your number to deliver OTP messages and secure your account. We do not sell phone numbers. See our Privacy Policy.
