SMS / OTP Terms

Last updated: August 13, 2026

These terms cover text messages and one-time passcodes (OTP) used to sign in to CuePoint Planning. They supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Program description

CuePoint Planning may send SMS messages containing one-time verification codes so you can create an account or sign in without a password. Messages are transactional authentication messages, not marketing blasts.

2. Consent

By providing your mobile number and choosing SMS authentication, you consent to receive automated text messages from CuePoint Planning (and our authentication providers) for account verification and security. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods, but SMS login will not work without a reachable number.

3. Frequency

Message frequency varies. You typically receive a message when you request a sign-in or signup code, and occasionally for related security notices.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. CuePoint Planning does not charge a separate SMS fee; your carrier may.

5. Opt out

You can stop SMS authentication messages by replying STOP to a message from our sender, or by switching your account to email OTP and contacting support@cuepointplanning.com to remove your phone number. Reply HELP for help.

After opting out, SMS login may stop working until you re-consent.

6. Supported carriers

Delivery is not guaranteed on all carriers or in all regions. We are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7. Privacy

We use your phone number to send OTP messages and secure your account. See our Privacy Policy. We do not sell phone numbers.
