Electronic Signatures Notice

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · ESIGN / UETA

CuePoint Planning’s client portal lets DJs and their clients sign contracts electronically. This notice explains what that means.
CuePoint Planning provides software tools only. We are not a law firm and are not a party to contracts between DJs and their clients. Have counsel review your contract templates for your state and event type.

1. Consent to electronic records and signatures

By using the portal signing flow, you agree to conduct the transaction electronically and that your electronic signature (typed name, click-to-sign, or similar affirmation) may be used to sign the displayed agreement.

2. Legal effect

Under the U.S. Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN) and state Uniform Electronic Transactions Acts (UETA), electronic signatures and records can have the same legal effect as paper signatures when parties consent and applicable requirements are met.

3. What we record

When a contract is signed in the portal, CuePoint may store signer name, timestamp, IP/user-agent where available, and signature affirmation metadata with the contract record for the DJ account.

4. Paper copies

You may request a copy of a signed contract from the DJ who sent it, or export/print from the portal where the feature is available. If you withdraw consent to electronic dealings, do not use the e-sign feature; contact the other party to use another signing method.

5. DJ responsibilities

  • Ensure contract content is accurate and lawful
  • Obtain any extra consents your jurisdiction requires
  • Do not use e-sign for documents that must be notarized or that law requires on paper
