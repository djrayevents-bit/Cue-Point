How CuePoint Planning LLC uses cookies and similar technologies (including localStorage) on cuepointplanning.com.
Legal Center Privacy Terms

1. What we mean by “cookies”

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage so the app can keep you signed in and load your workspace.

2. What we use

CategoryPurposeCan you disable?
Essential Sign-in sessions, security, routing, and core product function (including Supabase auth tokens and hosting/CDN cookies) No — the app will not work correctly without them
Functional storage Cache workspace data and UI preferences in the browser for performance Yes, by clearing site data (you will be signed out)
Analytics Only if we enable a measurement tool later to understand traffic and trial conversion Will be disclosed here before use

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party ad tracking pixels.

3. How to control them

Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking essential cookies will prevent login. Clearing storage signs you out and may reset local preferences.

4. Updates

If we add analytics or other non-essential technologies, we will update this policy (and add a consent prompt where required).
