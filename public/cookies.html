Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how CuePoint Planning LLC uses cookies and similar technologies such as localStorage on cuepointplanning.com.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, and pixels. We use these primarily to keep you signed in and to make the app work.

2. How We Use Them

CategoryPurposeExamples
Essential Authentication, security, load balancing, core app function Supabase session tokens; hosting/CDN cookies
Functional storage Cache app data for performance and offline-friendly UX Browser localStorage for CRM caches and UI preferences
Analytics (if enabled) Understand traffic and conversion (only if we enable a tool) First-party or privacy-respecting analytics — disclosed if added

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party ad tracking pixels on the marketing site or app.

3. Managing Preferences

Essential cookies are required for login and security; the product will not work correctly without them. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Clearing data will sign you out.

4. More Information

See our Privacy Policy for broader data practices, or email support@cuepointplanning.com.
