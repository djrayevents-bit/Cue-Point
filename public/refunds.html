Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: August 13, 2026

How CuePoint Planning LLC handles trials, cancellations, Founder pricing, and refund requests. Part of our Terms of Service.
Legal Center Terms Contact

1. How to cancel

Cancel anytime from Settings → Billing (Stripe Customer Portal) or email support@cuepointplanning.com. Cancellation stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the current paid period — or until the trial ends if you cancel during trial before conversion.

2. Free trial

If you cancel before the trial ends, you should not be charged for the following subscription period. Confirm status in the Billing Portal. If a charge posts in error, contact support within 7 days with your account email and Stripe receipt.

3. After a paid period starts

Fees are generally non-refundable for partial months. We may consider a case-by-case refund if a platform defect made the service substantially unusable, you report it within 7 days of the charge, and we cannot reasonably restore service.

Approved refunds go to the original payment method via Stripe and may take several business days to appear.

4. Founder pricing

The $20/mo Founder rate (first 50 subscribers) continues only while the subscription stays active and in good standing. Cancelling or letting it lapse may forfeit Founder pricing on resubscribe.

5. Chargebacks

Contact support before filing a chargeback so we can help. Unresolved chargebacks may lead to account suspension.

6. Payments between DJs and their clients

This policy covers CuePoint subscriptions only. Deposits or balances your event clients pay you are between you and your client. Online client card pay in the portal is not generally available until we announce it.

7. Contact

Billing: support@cuepointplanning.com
