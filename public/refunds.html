Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy explains how CuePoint Planning LLC handles trials, cancellations, Founder pricing, and refund requests. It is part of our Terms of Service.

1. How to cancel

Cancel anytime from Settings → Billing (Stripe Customer Portal) or by emailing support@cuepointplanning.com. Cancellation stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the current paid period (or trial end, if you cancel during trial before conversion).

2. Free trial

If you start a 30-day trial and cancel before the trial ends, you should not be charged for the subscription period that would have followed — confirm status in the Billing Portal. If a charge posts in error, contact support within 7 days with your account email and Stripe receipt.

3. Refunds after a paid period starts

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable for partial months. We may consider a goodwill or technical-issue refund case-by-case if:

  • A platform defect made the service substantially unusable, and
  • You report it within 7 days of the charge, and
  • We cannot reasonably restore service

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe and may take several business days to appear.

4. Founder pricing

Founder rate ($20/mo for the first 50 subscribers) continues only while the subscription remains active and in good standing. Cancelling or letting the subscription lapse may forfeit Founder pricing on resubscribe.

5. Chargebacks

Please contact support before filing a chargeback so we can help. Unresolved chargebacks may lead to account suspension.

6. Client payments (DJ ↔ client)

CuePoint’s subscription refund policy does not govern deposits or balances your event clients pay you. Until online client card pay is generally available, those payments are between you and your client (Venmo, Zelle, check, etc.).

7. Contact

Billing help: support@cuepointplanning.com
